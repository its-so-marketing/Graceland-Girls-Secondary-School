§01 — Our Story

A school built for the long term

Graceland opened in January 2009 in Gulu, the largest town in Northern Uganda. We were founded on a simple premise: girls here deserve a secondary school that takes them seriously — qualified teachers, a real campus, and the accreditation to sit them for national exams with confidence.

"Not the biggest school in Gulu — a dependable one, cohort after cohort."

Northern Uganda has spent two decades rebuilding. Schools that stay open, staffed, and well-run make a disproportionate difference here.

Ministry of Education & Sports Licensed
Registered with UNEB

§02 — Academics

Taught by people who know it well

Our 6.3-acre campus is built around one job: teaching well, every day, for four years straight. We hire trained, qualified teachers and expect them to lead — not just deliver a syllabus, but help each student think and work independently.
