About Graceland

A nurturing home for ambitious young women

Graceland Girls' Secondary School has stood in the heart of Gulu since January 2009. Our teachers are chosen not only for what they know, but for how they teach — with patience, structure, and a genuine investment in every student's progress.

With just over 400 students, we've kept our school small enough to know each girl by name, while building a foundation solid enough to carry her ambitions well beyond our gates.
