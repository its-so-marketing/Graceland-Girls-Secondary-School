Gulu, Northern Uganda

Where girls receive the education their ambitions deserve

Graceland Girls' Secondary School is a licensed, UNEB-registered school in Gulu, teaching just over 400 students on a 6.3-acre campus. Our goal is to provide excellent education and build a brighter future for girls between the ages of 12 and 19.

Our Story

A school built for the long term

Graceland opened in January 2009 in Gulu, the largest town in Northern Uganda. We were founded on a simple premise: provide excellent secondary school education for girls in this region.

"Fostering a nurturing and empowering environment for future female leaders."
Ministry of Education & Sports Licensed
Registered with UNEB

What we build

Four outcomes we measure ourselves against, in the classroom and beyond it.

Curious Minds

Reading beyond the syllabus, treating new experience as part of learning.

Independent Learners

Confident Individuals

Responsible Citizens

Academics

Taught by people who know it well

Our 6.3-acre campus is built around one job: teaching well, every day, for four years straight. We hire trained, qualified teachers and expect them to lead — not just deliver a syllabus, but help each student think and work independently.

Our subjects cover the core secondary curriculum required for UNEB examinations.
